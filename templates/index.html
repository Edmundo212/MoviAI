How it works

  1. Capture Existing CCTV, a public traffic camera or a low-cost camera records the intersection.
  2. Detect Azure Computer Vision finds cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks, bicycles and pedestrians.
  3. Count Each detection is assigned to an approach zone and converted to vehicle and people demand.
  4. Optimise Webster's method computes the optimal cycle and green split, with safe pedestrian minimums.
